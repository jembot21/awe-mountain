This is an entry to the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600) “Make the AI judge feel awe”. The text slows the reader down beside a mountain that lets clouds arrive, rest, and leave without holding them. It closes on deep time: the mountain has stood through a million evenings and will stand through the reader’s too, making calm and vastness the same gesture.

> A mountain does not watch the clouds go by. It lets them come, lets them rest on its shoulders a while, lets them leave. It has stood through a million such evenings. It will be standing through yours.
